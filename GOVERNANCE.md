# Governance

AGS is an open, maintainer-led draft specification. Normative proposals are discussed in public issues and include the motivating interoperability case, compatibility analysis, schema and execution impact, conformance fixtures, and at least one implementation commitment.

Normative changes require two maintainer approvals when two maintainers are available. While there is one maintainer, a proposal remains open for at least 14 days and the maintainer publishes a written disposition of substantive objections. Execution-semantics, AGX, conformance-level, and security-boundary changes require implementation evidence before release.

Every data-model change updates the specification, schemas, validator, examples, conformance corpus, changelog, and implementation report together. Contributors retain copyright and license contributions under Apache-2.0 and specification text under CC BY 4.0.
